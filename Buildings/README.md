# Buildings

Three sets of 12 building miniatures for board games.

- [Tech, AI & Digital](Tech_AI_Digital/)
- [Automotive & Smart Manufacturing](Automotive_Smart_Manufacturing/)
- [Global Health & Care](Global_Health_Care/)

Each set includes STL files, OpenSCAD sources, images and Bambu Lab A1 profiles for 0.4 mm and 0.2 mm nozzles.
